How this calculator works

The projected basic pay is calculated using your selected assumed fitment factor. HRA is then estimated using the HRA percentage you enter. Projected DA is shown as zero by default because the future 8th CPC DA rate is not known; you can adapt the tool later when official rules are published.

Why use an assumed fitment factor?

The fitment factor is a key variable in pay-revision projections. Since the final 8th CPC recommendation is not yet an official published pay matrix, ToolTomato lets you test different assumptions instead of presenting speculation as fact.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the official 8th Pay Commission calculator?

No. It is an independent projection tool. Official 8th CPC figures should be used once the Government publishes the applicable recommendations and rules.

Can I change the fitment factor?

Yes. Enter any assumption you want and compare the projected result.

Does this calculate final in-hand salary?

No. It estimates gross salary from the inputs provided. Deductions such as NPS, CGHS, income tax and other recoveries are not included.
