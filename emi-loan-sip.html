EMI / Loan / SIP Calculator

Plan a loan EMI, estimate the loan amount supported by a monthly EMI, or estimate SIP maturity value.

Loan and SIP results are estimates. Actual loan terms, fees, taxes, market returns and investment outcomes can differ. Do not treat estimated SIP returns as guaranteed.

How the EMI calculator works

For a standard reducing-balance loan, EMI depends on the principal, monthly interest rate and number of monthly payments.

EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ ÷ ((1+r)ⁿ − 1)

How the SIP calculator works

The SIP estimate uses a monthly investment and an assumed annual return converted to a monthly rate. It is a mathematical estimate, not a promise of investment performance.

Frequently asked questions

What does EMI mean?

EMI means Equated Monthly Instalment, the regular payment made toward a loan under a specified repayment schedule.

Is SIP return guaranteed?

No. The SIP calculator uses an assumed return rate. Actual investment returns can be higher or lower and are not guaranteed.

Can I use this for home or personal loans?

Yes, the EMI calculation can be used as a general estimate for loans that use a standard reducing-balance repayment method.
