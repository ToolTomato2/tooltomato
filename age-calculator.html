How to use the Age Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth.
  2. Choose the date you want to calculate your age as of.
  3. Tap Calculate Age.
  4. Your exact age, next birthday and other date-based results will appear instantly.

What does the calculator show?

The main result gives your age in complete years, months and days. It also shows your next birthday, the number of days until that birthday, approximate total months and the total number of elapsed days between the two dates.

Frequently asked questions

How is exact age calculated?

The calculator compares your birth date with the selected calculation date and accounts for different month lengths and leap years.

Can I calculate age for a past date?

Yes. Select any valid date after your date of birth in the “Calculate age as of” field.

Does the calculator work for leap-year birthdays?

Yes. The calculation handles leap years and February dates using calendar-based date arithmetic.
