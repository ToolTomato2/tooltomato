About ToolTomato

Simple tools, no friction.

ToolTomato was built with one idea in mind: everyday calculations and file tasks shouldn't need an account, a download, or your data leaving your device.

Every tool on this site runs directly in your browser. Where possible, files you work with (images, PDFs) are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to a server.

What you'll find here

  • Everyday calculators — age, percentage, discount, BMI, GST and more
  • Financial calculators — EMI, loan, SIP
  • Image tools — compress and resize
  • PDF tools — convert, merge, compress
  • Small utilities — word counter, QR code generator

Our approach

Results from calculators are estimates meant for quick, everyday use — see our Disclaimer for details, especially for financial tools.
