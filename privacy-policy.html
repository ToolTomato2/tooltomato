Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ToolTomato ("we", "us") handles information when you use tooltomato.com (the "Site").

  • Usage data: pages visited, browser type, approximate location and similar analytics data, collected via tools like Google Analytics.

What we don't collect

Files you process with our image or PDF tools (e.g. photos, PDFs) are handled directly in your browser and are not uploaded to or stored on our servers, unless a specific tool states otherwise on its page.

How we use information

Third-party services

We may use third-party services such as Google Analytics and Google AdSense, which may set their own cookies and collect data per their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings and through the cookie consent banner shown on this Site.

Children's privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
