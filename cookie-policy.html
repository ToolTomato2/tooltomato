What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites remember information about your visit.

Types of cookies we use

  • Essential cookies: needed for basic site functionality.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand how visitors use the Site (e.g. Google Analytics).
  • Advertising cookies: used, once ads are enabled, by providers like Google AdSense to show relevant ads and measure performance.

Managing cookies

You can accept or decline non-essential cookies via the banner shown on the Site, and you can clear or block cookies at any time through your browser settings.
