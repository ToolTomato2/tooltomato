How to resize an image

  1. Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Enter your target width and height in pixels.
  3. Keep aspect ratio enabled to avoid stretching, or disable it for custom dimensions.
  4. Choose an output format and tap Resize Image.
  5. Preview and download your resized image.

Private browser-based resizing

The image is processed locally in your browser. The tool does not need to upload your photo to a server.

Frequently asked questions

Will resizing improve image quality?

No. Resizing changes dimensions. Enlarging a small image may make it look softer, while reducing dimensions can reduce file size.

What does maintain aspect ratio mean?

It keeps the original width-to-height proportion so the image is less likely to look stretched or squashed.

Can I resize images on my phone?

Yes. The tool is designed to work in modern mobile and desktop browsers.
