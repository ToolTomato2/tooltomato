Note: this version truly recompresses rendered PDF pages. Text-heavy PDFs can sometimes become larger because pages are rebuilt as images. If that happens, the tool will tell you and offer the smaller file.

How it works

Each page is rendered in your browser and rebuilt as a JPEG at a level-specific resolution and quality. This gives Basic, Moderate and Strong modes a real compression difference instead of only changing PDF metadata.

Privacy

Your selected PDF is processed locally in your browser. It is not intentionally uploaded to a ToolTomato server.
